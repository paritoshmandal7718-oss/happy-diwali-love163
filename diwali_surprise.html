For Anurupa, with the warmest wishes

May Your World Always Glow.

May this Diwali bring you the kind of happiness that stays long after the lights fade — peaceful mornings, beautiful surprises, genuine smiles, and countless reasons to feel proud of yourself.

I pray that every effort you make finds its way to success. May your dreams grow bigger, your confidence grow stronger, and every new chapter bring you closer to the life you wish for.

When days feel difficult, may you find courage; when good things arrive, may you have a hundred reasons to celebrate.

May life always be gentle with your heart. May you feel valued, respected, supported, and loved. You deserve a future filled with light, calm, laughter, and beautiful possibilities.

Shubho Deepaboli, Anurupa. ✨

A little prayer for us

May Our Light Grow Together.

Tonight, may our prayer be simple: let there always be honesty in our words, patience in our silences, kindness in our disagreements, and gratitude in the little moments we share.

May we keep choosing understanding over ego, trust over doubt, and care over careless words. May we encourage each other's dreams without ever dimming our own.

May every step forward bring more respect, warmth, and a deeper appreciation for one another. May time teach us to be even more thoughtful.

Here's to growing closer through loyalty, gentleness, laughter, and the comfort of knowing we wish the best for each other.

May the light guide both our paths. 🪔

With respect, from both our hearts

For The People Who Mean Home.

On this beautiful Diwali, our warmest wishes, love, and heartfelt pronam to both our families — to our parents and everyone who has cared for us, guided us, and helped us become who we are.

May our two families be blessed with good health, peace, happiness, and togetherness. May every home be filled with laughter, every heart with hope.

With immense respect and gratitude, we send our love to our second parents too. May they always feel the warmth and affection we hold for them.

Pronam, Love & Endless Good Wishes. ❤️
